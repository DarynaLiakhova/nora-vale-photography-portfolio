About the photographer

Nora Vale

Photographer · Bratislava

I photograph people as they are — present, unguarded and fully inside their own story.

I’m an independent photographer based in Bratislava and working across Europe. My work moves between intimate weddings, natural portraits and thoughtful editorial commissions.

I keep the process calm and unobtrusive, offering gentle direction only when it helps. The result is imagery that feels refined without losing the honest moments that made the day yours.
